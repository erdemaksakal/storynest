What StoryNest is

StoryNest is an iOS app that helps families turn short spoken or typed stories into picture books. Free users can create unlimited text books on supported devices using Apple Intelligence. Optional StoryNest Pro unlocks illustrated books with premium AI. The app may also deliver pre-made daily gift books and local notifications, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Common troubleshooting

  • Update the app — Install the latest version from the App Store.
  • Restart the app — Close it from the app switcher and open it again.
  • Restart your device — Resolves many temporary audio, network, or permission issues.
  • Check permissions — In Settings → StoryNest, confirm Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed if you want to tell stories by voice.
  • Check notifications — In Settings → StoryNest → Notifications, allow alerts if you want reminders about daily gift books.
  • Network connection — Illustrated books, your welcome book, and daily gift downloads require internet. Text-only books on supported devices can work without cloud generation.
  • Apple Intelligence — Free text books need a compatible device and Apple Intelligence enabled in system settings. If unavailable, the app may use a limited local fallback.
  • Pro subscription — Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases if you subscribed but Pro features are missing. Confirm you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to subscribe. For billing questions, contact Apple Support or manage the subscription in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Daily Pro limit — Pro includes a limited number of illustrated books per day. If you reach the limit, try again the next day.
  • Reinstall — If problems persist, delete the app and reinstall. Local books and preferences stored on your device will be removed; Pro access can be restored with the same Apple ID.
