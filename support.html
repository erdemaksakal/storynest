What StoryNest is

StoryNest is an iOS app that helps families turn short spoken or typed stories into picture books. You can switch a book between languages, learn English words from your stories, practice pronunciation for points, unlock gift books, receive a daily gift story, and share books to Instagram, Facebook, or PDF. Free users can create unlimited text books on supported devices using Apple Intelligence. Optional StoryNest Pro unlocks illustrated books with premium AI (currently up to 20 per month). Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Common troubleshooting

  • Update the app — Install the latest version from the App Store.
  • Restart the app — Close it from the app switcher and open it again.
  • Restart your device — Resolves many temporary audio, network, or permission issues.
  • Check permissions — In Settings → StoryNest, confirm Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed if you want to tell stories by voice or practice pronunciation.
  • Check notifications — In Settings → StoryNest → Notifications, allow alerts if you want reminders about daily gift books or a newly unlocked gift book.
  • Network connection — Illustrated books, your welcome book, language translation, daily gifts, and point-reward books require internet. Text-only books on supported devices can work without cloud generation.
  • Apple Intelligence — Free text books need a compatible device and Apple Intelligence enabled in system settings. If unavailable, the app may use a limited local fallback.
  • Translation — Use the language toggle on a book page (for example Turkish ↔ English). This needs a network connection.
  • Pronunciation & points — Practice words at the end of a book or in your word list. Speak clearly; every 500 points unlocks an illustrated gift book. Points are stored on your device together with quota records we need to grant the gift.
  • Sharing — Open a book and use Share to send Instagram, Facebook, or PDF. If a destination does not appear, check that the Instagram or Facebook app is installed, then try the iOS share sheet again.
  • Pro subscription — Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases if you subscribed but Pro features are missing. Confirm you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to subscribe. For billing questions, contact Apple Support or manage the subscription in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Monthly Pro limit — Pro includes up to 20 illustrated books per month. If you reach the limit, you can still create unlimited text-only books on device until the next period.
  • Reinstall — If problems persist, delete the app and reinstall. Local books and preferences stored on your device will be removed; Pro access can be restored with the same Apple ID.
