Privacy Policy

StoryNest · Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StoryNest (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our iOS application (“the app”). It is meant to be read together with our Terms of Use and any in-app legal or subscription disclosures.

1. What StoryNest does

StoryNest helps children and families turn short spoken or typed stories into illustrated picture books. You can record a story with the microphone, type text, and the app will format it into pages. Depending on your plan and device, books may use on-device Apple Intelligence (text and system symbols) or cloud AI for richer stories and AI-generated images. The app may also download pre-made daily gift books from our public content feed and send optional local notifications when a new gift is available.

2. Our approach to privacy

We do not operate a StoryNest login or account system. We do not run third-party advertising SDKs for behavioral tracking, and we do not sell your personal information. Our business model is a free app with optional StoryNest Pro subscriptions purchased through the App Store.

Because the app is used to create children’s stories, parents and guardians should supervise use of the app and review story content before sharing it with children.

3. Information we do not collect for marketing

We do not ask you to register a StoryNest account. We do not intentionally collect, store on servers we operate, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location for marketing or profiling. We do not track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

4. Data stored on your device

By default, your created books (text, metadata, and saved illustration files), app preferences, quota-related flags, and a random app identifier used for usage limits are stored locally on your device using iOS storage (for example the app’s documents directory and standard app settings).

You can delete individual books in the app, or remove local data by deleting the app (subject to your device and iOS settings).

5. Microphone, speech recognition, and story input

If you choose to tell a story by voice, the app requests access to the microphone and speech recognition, as described in the system permission prompts. Speech is converted to text so you can edit it and create a book. Speech recognition is provided through Apple’s Speech framework and is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. Depending on your device and settings, recognition may occur on device or involve Apple’s services.

We do not upload raw audio recordings to servers we operate for storage or training. The resulting text of your story may be processed further as described below when you create certain types of books.

6. On-device processing (free text books)

On supported devices, unlimited text-only books can be generated using Apple Intelligence on your iPhone or iPad. In that mode, story formatting is intended to stay on your device and does not require sending your story to our backend for generation. Illustrations in the free plan use system symbols rather than uploaded photos or cloud image generation.

On-device features are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices. If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, the app may use a limited local fallback generator so you can still preview a book.

7. Cloud processing (welcome book, Pro books, translations)

When you use features that rely on our cloud backend—for example your one-time welcome illustrated book, StoryNest Pro illustrated books, or translation of a daily gift book—the app sends relevant information to our servers hosted on Supabase, which may forward content to OpenAI to generate or translate story text and images.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Your story text (typed or transcribed from speech)
  • Book settings such as page count, language, and illustration style
  • Generated page text and illustration scene descriptions
  • A pseudonymous device identifier (a random ID stored on your device, not your Apple ID)
  • Subscription tier and quota-related flags needed to enforce plan limits

Our backend stores limited usage records tied to the device identifier (for example whether the welcome book was used and how many Pro books were created today). We use this to enforce plan limits, not to build marketing profiles.

OpenAI processes content according to its own policies. We configure these services to provide the feature you request; we do not use your stories to train our own models.

Learn more: Supabase Privacy, OpenAI Privacy Policy.

8. Daily gift books and public content

The app may download publicly hosted daily gift book files (JSON and related assets) from GitHub or similar content endpoints configured by us. These requests retrieve book content for the selected date and are not used to identify you personally. If your device language differs from the gift book language, the app may send the book text to our translation service as described above.

GitHub’s handling of technical connection data is governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

9. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may schedule local reminders about daily gift books. Notification permissions and delivery are handled by iOS. We do not use notifications to track you across apps.

10. Purchases (StoryNest Pro)

If you purchase a subscription or other in-app product, payment and subscription status are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card number. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to determine whether Pro features are active on your device.

11. Security

We design the app so your library stays on your device rather than on our infrastructure. Connections to our backend and content hosts use standard protections supported by iOS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; keep your device software up to date and supervise children’s use of the app.

12. Retention and your choices

Books and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Server-side usage records tied to your pseudonymous device identifier are kept only as long as needed to operate quotas and prevent abuse, after which they may be deleted or aggregated.

Because we do not maintain StoryNest user accounts, there is no separate “account deletion” with us—your primary controls are in the app, on your iPhone or iPad, and in your Apple ID / App Store settings.

13. Children’s privacy

StoryNest is designed for families to create stories for children, but it is intended to be used with parent or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction) through a dedicated child account, because the app does not offer one.

If a child’s story text is submitted for cloud generation, that content is treated as user-provided input for the feature requested by the device owner. Parents and guardians should review stories before creation and sharing. If you believe we have received information that raises a concern, please contact us using the details below.

14. International users and your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal data, or to object or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Most personal information stays on your device or with Apple (Speech, App Store). Limited server-side data relates mainly to pseudonymous device identifiers, usage quotas, and story content you choose to send for cloud features.

European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland: Where GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases for processing may include performance of our relationship with you (providing the app), legitimate interests (such as quota enforcement, security, and support), and compliance with law. You may contact us with requests or questions as set out below.

California and similar U.S. state laws: We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are commonly defined for “sale” of consumer data, and we do not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics about you for prohibited purposes. California residents may have rights to know, delete, or correct personal information held by a business, subject to exceptions. To exercise rights or ask questions, use the contact method below.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy, unless applicable law requires additional steps.

16. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how StoryNest handles data, contact us through the App Store listing for StoryNest (Support / developer contact) or the support channel listed there.
